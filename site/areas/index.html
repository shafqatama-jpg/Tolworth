Good to know

Area questions

Not sure if we cover you? Call 020 3143 1718 or WhatsApp 07301 498451.

Do you pick up from home in my area?

Yes. Pick-up and drop-off are free anywhere in the areas on this page — from home, school, college, work or a train station.

Is the price the same in every area?

Yes. Lessons are from £35 an hour on a 30-hour block, £40 for a single hour and £75 for a two-hour beginner lesson, wherever you live. Manual and automatic cost the same.

Which test centre will I use?

Most pupils test at Tolworth Driving Test Centre. If you live closer to Morden or Raynes Park, you can test at Morden instead and we’ll train you on those routes.

My area isn’t listed. Can you still help?

Probably. We cover roughly six miles around Tolworth (postcode KT6 4DU). Call 020 3143 1718 or WhatsApp 07301 498451 and we’ll tell you straight away.

Can I start lessons in one area and finish in another?

Yes. Many pupils start near home and move on to the roads around the test centre as they get closer to their test.
