Areas we cover around Tolworth

Driving lessons with free pick-up across Tolworth and everywhere within about six miles — from Kingston to Ewell and Esher to Morden.

Book a lessonWhatsApp us

We cover Tolworth (KT5, KT6) and all areas within about six miles, including Surbiton, Kingston, New Malden, Chessington, Worcester Park, Ewell, Stoneleigh, Hook, Esher, Claygate, Raynes Park and Morden (SM4). Pick-up and drop-off are free within this area.

Tolworth

KT5 / KT6

Our home patch — the Broadway, the A3 and the roads around the test centre.

Surbiton

KT6

Pick-ups from Surbiton station, Victoria Road and the residential roads off Ewell Road.

Berrylands

KT5

Quiet residential streets that are ideal for first lessons and manoeuvres.

Hook

KT9

The Hook roundabout and A243 for building roundabout confidence.

Chessington

KT9

Lessons around Chessington and the A243, with a short drive to Tolworth's test routes.

Long Ditton

KT6

Calm roads for beginners, close to Surbiton and Kingston.

Thames Ditton

KT7

Village roads and the Portsmouth Road, minutes from Surbiton.

Kingston upon Thames

KT1 / KT2

Town-centre driving, one-way systems and bus lanes for confident town driving.

New Malden

KT3

The A3 and busy local high street driving.

Motspur Park

KT3 / KT4

Residential roads between New Malden and Worcester Park.

Worcester Park

KT4

Central Road and the surrounding streets, close to the A3.

Stoneleigh

KT19 / KT17

Quiet roads near the A240 linking to Tolworth.

Ewell

KT17 / KT19

Ewell village, the bypass and the A240 towards Tolworth.

Esher

KT10

Esher high street and the A3 junctions to the south-west.

Claygate

KT10

Rural-feel lanes, useful practice for narrow roads.

Raynes Park

SW20

The A3 and busy junctions on Tolworth's north-east side.

Morden

SM4

SM4 — we also teach around Morden and its test centre.
