Automatic vs Manual: Which Should You Learn in 2026?

More learners than ever are choosing automatic. But is it right for you? Here's an honest comparison to help you decide.

By Kings Driving Centre · 23 November 2026 · 3 min read

More learners than ever are choosing automatic. But is it right for you? Here's an honest comparison to help you decide.

The licence difference

If you pass in a manual car, your licence lets you drive both manual and automatic cars. If you pass in an automatic, you can only drive automatics — you'd need to pass another test to drive a manual.

Why automatic is so popular

  • Less to think about. No clutch, no gears, no stalling — so more attention for the road.
  • Often quicker to learn. Many learners reach test standard sooner.
  • Easier in traffic. Stop-start roads like Tolworth Broadway are much less tiring.
  • The future. Hybrids and all electric cars are automatic, and more new cars are automatic every year.

If automatic means you need fewer lessons, it can work out cheaper overall.

Why some people still choose manual

  • Flexibility. One licence covers both types of car.
  • Cheaper cars. Used manual cars can be cheaper to buy.
  • Work or family needs. If you'll drive a manual van or a shared family car, manual makes sense.

Our cars

So which should you choose?

If you want the most flexible licence and don't mind more to learn, go manual. If you want to learn faster and plan to drive modern or electric cars, automatic is a great choice. Not sure? Book a first automatic lesson and see how it feels — you can always switch.

Frequently asked questions

Is the automatic test easier?

The test is identical — same length, same marking. Many people find it easier to reach test standard in an automatic.

Can I switch from manual to automatic lessons?

Yes. Your experience carries over and most people adapt within a few lessons.

Are automatic lessons more expensive?

Not with us — the price is the same for manual and automatic.
