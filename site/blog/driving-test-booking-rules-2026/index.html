Driving Test Booking Rules in 2026: What's Changed

DVSA has changed how car driving tests are booked and managed. Here's what the 2026 rules mean for you, in plain English.

By Kings Driving Centre · 23 November 2026 · 3 min read

DVSA has changed how car driving tests are booked and managed. Here's what the 2026 rules mean for you, in plain English.

Why the rules changed

DVSA introduced the changes to stop third-party services booking up test slots and reselling them at inflated prices. The aim is that learners only ever pay the official DVSA fee.

1. Only 2 changes per booking (since 31 March 2026)

You can now change your test date, time or location only twice. Once you've used both changes, you'd need to cancel and rebook — and cancelling resets the limit. So choose a date you realistically expect to be ready by.

Book only when you're close to test standard — and book on GOV.UK so you only pay the official fee.

2. Only the learner can book (since 12 May 2026)

Only you, the learner, can book, change, swap or cancel your car driving test. It's now against the law for anyone else — including your driving instructor — to do it for you. Your instructor can still help you choose a sensible date and test centre.

3. The 3 nearest centres rule (since 9 June 2026)

If you move your test, you can only move it to one of the 3 test centres nearest to where it's currently booked. You can still book any centre at first, so pick the one you genuinely intend to use.

What does a test cost?

DVSA charges £62 for a weekday test and £75 for evenings, weekends and bank holidays. Always book on GOV.UK so you only pay the official fee.

What it means for your lessons

Because changes are limited, it's more important than ever to book only when you're close to test standard. A mock test on the Tolworth roads is the best way to find out. If you've got a test booked and need a car, see our test cover.

Read DVSA's full guide to the 2026 booking rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can my instructor book my driving test?

No. Since 12 May 2026 only the learner can book or change their own car test.

How many times can I change my test?

Twice per booking. Cancelling and rebooking resets the limit.

Can I move my test to any centre?

Since 9 June 2026 you can only move a booked test to one of the 3 nearest centres to where it's booked.
