How Many Driving Lessons Do I Need?

It's the question every learner asks first. There's no single answer, but there is a sensible way to estimate it — and plenty you can do to need fewer.

By Kings Driving Centre · 23 November 2026 · 3 min read
ContentsThe official figureWhat affects how many you needA rough guideHow to need fewer lessonsWhat it costsFAQs

It's the question every learner asks first. There's no single answer, but there is a sensible way to estimate it — and plenty you can do to need fewer.

The official figure

DVSA says that learners who pass have had, on average, around 45 hours of professional lessons plus about 22 hours of private practice. That's an average across the country — plenty of people need fewer, and some need more.

What affects how many you need

  • Previous experience — even a little private practice helps.
  • Manual or automatic — many learners reach test standard sooner in an automatic.
  • How often you learn — regular lessons each week stop skills fading between sessions.
  • Lesson length — two-hour lessons give more real driving time.
  • Confidence and nerves — a calm learner usually progresses faster, which is why we never rush you.

A rough guide

As a very rough guide for our pupils: complete beginners often need 30 hours or more; learners with some experience 15 to 25; and drivers who've practised a lot privately may only need 8 to 15 to polish up for the test. Your instructor will give you an honest estimate after your first lesson or two.

How to need fewer lessons

  1. Have lessons regularly — at least once or twice a week.
  2. Choose two-hour lessons where you can.
  3. Add private practice with a family member, if you can be insured.
  4. Pass your theory test early so you understand the rules on the road.
  5. Take a mock test before booking your practical, so you only book when you're ready.
Instructor tip: Book your practical test for a date you realistically expect to be ready by — since 31 March 2026 you can only change it twice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pass with 10 lessons?

Some learners with lots of previous practice do, but most beginners need considerably more. We'll be honest with you about where you are.

Are automatic lessons quicker?

Many learners reach test standard sooner in an automatic because there's less to manage.

How often should I have lessons?

At least once or twice a week works well for most people. Intensive courses suit learners with a deadline.
