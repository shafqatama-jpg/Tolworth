What Happens on Test Day at Tolworth

Knowing exactly what will happen makes test day far less scary. Here's how it usually goes, from waking up to getting your result.

By Kings Driving Centre · 23 November 2026 · 3 min read

Knowing exactly what will happen makes test day far less scary. Here's how it usually goes, from waking up to getting your result.

The night before

Check your test time and centre, put your photocard provisional licence somewhere safe, and get a proper night's sleep. If you wear glasses or contact lenses for driving, have them ready.

Your warm-up lesson

If you're using our car, your instructor will pick you up for a warm-up lesson first. It's a chance to settle your nerves, practise a manoeuvre and drive some of the local roads before you arrive.

At the test centre

Arrive a few minutes early. The examiner will check your licence and ask you to sign a declaration. You can ask for your instructor to sit in on the test — many pupils find it reassuring.

The eyesight check and tell me question

You'll read a number plate from 20 metres. Then, before you drive, the examiner asks one "tell me" vehicle safety question. Practise them all with our flip cards.

Instructor tip: Mistakes happen. If you think you've made one, don't dwell on it — it may not even be marked. Focus on the next hazard.

The drive

You'll drive for about 40 minutes on a mix of roads, including around 20 minutes of independent driving with a sat nav or following signs. You'll do one manoeuvre, may be asked to do an emergency stop, and will get one "show me" question while driving.

Your result

Back at the centre, the examiner tells you whether you've passed and talks you through any faults. Pass with 15 or fewer driving faults and no serious or dangerous faults, and you can usually have your full licence sent to you automatically.

If it doesn't go your way

It happens to lots of good drivers. Listen to the feedback, and you can rebook after 10 working days. We'll focus your next lessons on exactly what went wrong.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to bring to my driving test?

Your photocard provisional licence, and glasses or contact lenses if you need them. Check GOV.UK for the latest requirements.

Can my instructor sit in on my test?

Yes, if you'd like them to.

How soon can I rebook if I fail?

You can take another test after 10 working days.
