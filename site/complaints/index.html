Complaints

Last updated: November 2026

We want every lesson to be a good experience. If something hasn't gone right, please tell us so we can put it right.

How to complain

  1. Speak to your instructor if you feel comfortable — many issues can be sorted out quickly.
  2. Or contact us directly: email info@kingsdrivingcentre.co.uk or call 020 3143 1718. Please include your name, the date of the lesson and what happened.
  3. We'll acknowledge your complaint, look into it fairly and reply with what we'll do.

If you're still not satisfied

All our instructors are DVSA Approved Driving Instructors. If you have a concern about an instructor's conduct or standard of instruction that we haven't resolved, you can report it to DVSA.
