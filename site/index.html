FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much are driving lessons in Tolworth?

Lessons are £40 for one hour or £75 for a two-hour beginner lesson. Block bookings bring the price down to £35 an hour with a 30-hour block. Prices are the same for manual and automatic.

Are you a franchise?

No. Tolworth Driving School is part of Kings Driving Centre, an independent local driving school based in Surbiton. Our instructors live and teach locally.

Do you teach manual and automatic?

Yes, both, at the same price. Our fleet includes Toyota Yaris, Toyota Yaris Cross and VW T-Cross, all dual-controlled.

Which areas do you cover?

Tolworth and everywhere within about six miles, including Surbiton, Kingston, New Malden, Chessington, Worcester Park, Ewell, Esher, Claygate and Morden (SM4).

Can I take my test in your car?

Yes. Test cover is £130 for our pupils or £150 at short notice, including a warm-up lesson. You need to be test-ready.

How do I pay?

All major debit and credit cards including American Express, or spread the cost with Klarna. There are no card fees.
