3Theory & hazard perception

Pass your theory first time.

Little and often

Fifteen minutes of practice questions every day beats a weekend of cramming. Use the official DVSA practice materials.

Learn the why

Understanding why a rule exists (not just the answer) makes the questions much easier — and makes you a safer driver.

Hazard perception

Click as soon as you see a hazard starting to develop — like a pedestrian stepping towards the kerb. Don't click wildly; patterns are penalised.

Use real lessons

Ask your instructor to talk through hazards on the road. Spotting them for real makes the video clips feel familiar.

Practise on GOV.UK

Take a free practice theory test to see where you are.

Book when ready

Book your theory test on GOV.UK only, so you pay the official fee.

Questions about learning to drive

When can I start driving lessons?

You can start lessons on public roads from 17 with a provisional licence. You can apply for the licence from 15.

Can my driving instructor book my test for me?

Not any more. Since 12 May 2026 only the learner can book, change or cancel their own car driving test on GOV.UK. We can still help you choose a sensible date.

How many times can I change my driving test?

Since 31 March 2026 you can make 2 changes to a booking. From 9 June 2026 you can only move a test to one of the 3 test centres nearest to where it's booked.

How much does the practical test cost?

DVSA charges £62 for a weekday test and £75 for evenings, weekends and bank holidays — always book on GOV.UK so you only pay the official fee.
