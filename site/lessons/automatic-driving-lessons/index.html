Automatic driving lessons in Tolworth cost the same as manual: £40 for one hour, £75 for a two-hour beginner lesson, or from £35 an hour on a 30-hour block. You learn in a modern dual-controlled automatic with reversing and 360 cameras.

Why so many learners choose automatic

Without a clutch or gears to manage, you can put all your attention on what matters most: reading the road, planning ahead and making good decisions. Most learners reach test standard faster in an automatic, and the car is far less tiring in Tolworth's stop-start traffic.

The one limit is that an automatic licence only covers automatic cars. With most new cars, hybrids and all electric cars now automatic, that matters less every year.

Our automatic cars

We teach in modern, dual-controlled Toyota cars in Kings Driving Centre livery, fitted with reversing cameras, 360 cameras and parking sensors. They're comfortable, easy to see out of and insured specifically for driving tuition.

What you'll learn

  • Safe control: moving off, stopping, speed control and steering
  • Junctions, crossroads and the big local roundabouts
  • Dual carriageway driving on the A3 Kingston bypass
  • All test manoeuvres, including parallel parking and bay parking
  • Independent driving with a sat nav, as on the real test
  • Show me / tell me questions and full mock tests on Tolworth routes

Nervous about starting? Our nervous driver lessons start in quiet roads and build at your pace.

Frequently asked questions

Is an automatic driving test easier?

The test itself is identical — same length, same marking. Many learners find it easier to reach test standard in an automatic because there's less to manage.

Can I switch from manual to automatic?

Yes. Your experience carries over, so most people only need a few lessons to get used to an automatic.

Can I take my test in your automatic car?

Yes, as long as your instructor agrees you're test-ready. See our test cover page for details.
