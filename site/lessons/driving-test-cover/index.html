Driving test cover at Tolworth

Got a test booked at Tolworth? Take it in our dual-controlled car, after a warm-up on the local routes with an instructor who knows them inside out.

Book a lesson →WhatsApp us

Driving test cover at Tolworth costs £130 for our regular pupils and £150 for urgent or short-notice bookings. It includes a pre-test warm-up lesson and use of our car for the test. You must be test-ready.

What's included

  • Pick-up and a warm-up lesson before your test, covering the routes and junctions you're most likely to meet
  • Use of our dual-controlled, fully insured car for the test itself
  • Your instructor can sit in on the test if you'd like
  • A debrief afterwards, whatever the result

Test-ready policy

Before we confirm test cover, we'll want to see you drive. If your instructor believes you aren't yet safe or test-ready, we may decline and recommend more lessons first. It's not a sales tactic — failing a test costs you the test fee and weeks of waiting.

Short-notice and cancellation tests

Picked up a cancellation slot at short notice? Get in touch straight away by phone or WhatsApp and we'll do our best to cover it. For round-the-clock urgent cover, see Tolworth Driving Centre, our dedicated short-notice service.

Our instructors keep track of local roadworks and closures so your warm-up reflects how the area looks on the day.

Areas we cover

We pick up across Tolworth and around 6 miles of it, including Surbiton, Kingston, New Malden, Chessington, Worcester Park, Ewell, Esher and Morden. See all areas.

Frequently asked questions

How much notice do you need?

As much as possible, but we regularly cover tests booked at short notice. Message us on WhatsApp with your test date, time and centre.

Do you cover tests at other centres?

Our focus is Tolworth, and we can often cover Morden too. Ask us about other nearby centres.

What do I need to bring to my test?

Your photocard provisional licence and your theory test pass details. Check the latest requirements on GOV.UK before your test.
