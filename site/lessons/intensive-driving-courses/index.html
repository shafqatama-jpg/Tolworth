Is an intensive course right for you?

Intensive courses work best if you can commit to several hours of driving a week, learn well in focused blocks, and have (or can get) a practical test date. They suit learners with a deadline — a new job, university or a move — and people who already have some experience and want to finish off.

If you're a complete beginner, a semi-intensive course over a few weeks usually gives better results than cramming everything into a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Do you book the driving test for me?

You'll need to book your own practical test on GOV.UK, but we'll help you choose dates that fit your course.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, you can spread the cost with Klarna. We also accept all major cards including American Express.

What if I'm not ready by my test date?

Your instructor will tell you honestly. We'll only take you to test in our car if you're test-ready — it protects your money and your first-time pass chances.
