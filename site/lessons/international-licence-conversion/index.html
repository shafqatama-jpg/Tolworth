Driving lessons for international licence holders

Experienced driver, new to UK roads? We'll help you adapt to driving on the left and prepare for the UK test if you need to take one.

Book a lesson →WhatsApp us

Lessons for international licence holders in Tolworth cost £40 per hour. They focus on UK road rules, roundabouts and test standard driving. Whether you can exchange your licence or need to pass a UK test depends on the country it was issued in.

Exchange or test?

Licences from some countries can be exchanged for a UK licence; others can be used for up to 12 months after you become resident, after which you must pass the UK theory and practical tests. Check your situation on GOV.UK.

What we cover

  • Driving on the left, roundabouts and lane discipline
  • UK road signs, markings and speed limits
  • Pedestrian crossings, bus lanes and box junctions
  • The UK test format, manoeuvres and show me / tell me questions
  • Mock tests on Tolworth routes

Most experienced drivers need far fewer lessons than a beginner. We'll assess your driving first and give you an honest plan.

Areas we cover

We pick up across Tolworth and around 6 miles of it, including Surbiton, Kingston, New Malden, Chessington, Worcester Park, Ewell, Esher and Morden. See all areas.

Frequently asked questions

How many lessons will I need?

Experienced drivers often need between five and fifteen hours, depending on how different UK driving is from what you're used to.

Do I need a provisional licence?

If you need to take the UK test, yes — you'll apply for a provisional licence, then take the theory and practical tests.

Can you help with test cover?

Yes. See our test cover page.
