Manual driving lessons in Tolworth

Learn to drive a manual car with a patient local instructor, on the same roads you'll be tested on at Tolworth.

Book a lesson →WhatsApp us

Manual driving lessons in Tolworth cost £40 for one hour or £75 for a two-hour beginner lesson, falling to £35 an hour with a 30-hour block. Lessons are one-to-one in a dual-controlled car with free pick-up across KT5, KT6 and nearby areas.

Why learn manual in Tolworth?

A manual licence lets you drive both manual and automatic cars, which is still useful if you plan to share a family car, drive for work or hire cars abroad. The trade-off is that there's more to master, especially clutch control on hills and at busy junctions.

Tolworth is a good place to learn it properly. Within a few minutes of the test centre you get slow, queuing traffic on the Broadway, fast dual-carriageway driving on the A3, roundabouts of every size and quiet residential streets for manoeuvres. We use all of it, step by step, so nothing on test day is new.

How our manual lessons work

  • Clutch and gears first, in quiet roads. Moving off, stopping, hill starts and smooth gear changes until they become automatic.
  • Junctions and roundabouts next. Building to the Tolworth and Hook roundabouts and the A3 slip roads once you're ready.
  • Test standard driving. Independent driving with a sat nav, the manoeuvres, show me / tell me questions and mock tests on local routes.

Your instructor tracks your progress on an iPad against the DVSA syllabus, so you always know what's next and how close you are to test standard.

Who it suits

Manual lessons suit learners who want the most flexible licence, people who already have some experience in a manual car, and anyone who'd rather pay less for a car later (manual used cars are usually cheaper). If you find the clutch stressful, have a look at automatic lessons — many of our pupils switch and pass sooner.

Areas we cover

We pick up across Tolworth and around 6 miles of it, including Surbiton, Kingston, New Malden, Chessington, Worcester Park, Ewell, Esher and Morden. See all areas.

Frequently asked questions

How many manual lessons will I need?

It varies a lot from person to person. The DVSA's guidance is that learners who pass have around 45 hours of lessons plus 22 hours of private practice. Your instructor will give you an honest estimate after your first couple of lessons.

Do you pick up from home?

Yes. We pick up and drop off at home, college, work or Surbiton and Tolworth stations within our area at no extra cost.

Is the price different for manual and automatic?

No. Our prices are the same for manual and automatic lessons.
