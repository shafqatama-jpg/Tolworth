Mock driving tests on Tolworth test routes

Find out if you're ready before the real thing. A full mock test on the roads around Tolworth Test Centre, marked to DVSA standards.

Book a lessonWhatsApp us

A mock driving test in Tolworth costs £80 for two hours. It includes a full test simulation on local routes, marked like the real test, followed by a detailed debrief and practice on any weak areas.

What happens on a mock test

  1. Eyesight check and a tell me question, just like the start of the real test.
  2. Around 40 minutes of driving on roads used for Tolworth tests, including independent driving with a sat nav.
  3. One manoeuvre and a show me question while driving.
  4. Full marking — driving faults, serious and dangerous faults — then a clear debrief.
  5. Practice time on anything that came up.

When to take one

We recommend a mock test around four weeks before your real test, so there's time to fix anything it shows up. Many learners take a second one a few days before test day.

Areas we cover

We pick up across Tolworth and around 6 miles of it, including Surbiton, Kingston, New Malden, Chessington, Worcester Park, Ewell, Esher and Morden. See all areas.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a mock test if I learn with another school?

Yes. It's a good way to get a second opinion before test day.

Will the mock test be on the real test routes?

We use the roads and junctions most often used on Tolworth tests. DVSA doesn't publish exact routes, and they change, so we focus on the areas you're most likely to meet.

Is it in a manual or automatic?

Either — the price is the same.
