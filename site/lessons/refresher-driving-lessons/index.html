Refresher driving lessons in Tolworth

Passed a while ago but lost your confidence? Refresher lessons get you back on the road, at your pace and on your terms.

Book a lesson →WhatsApp us

Refresher driving lessons in Tolworth cost £40 per hour, manual or automatic. They're for full licence holders who want to rebuild confidence, tackle motorways or busy roads, or get used to driving in the UK.

Who takes refresher lessons?

  • Drivers who passed years ago and haven't driven since
  • New parents and people returning after a break or illness
  • Drivers who avoid motorways, roundabouts or parking
  • Drivers new to UK roads (see international licence conversion)
  • Anyone moving from manual to automatic, or to a new or electric car

Built around you

There's no syllabus to follow. We start with a relaxed assessment drive, ask what worries you, and plan lessons around it — whether that's the A3 at rush hour, the Tolworth roundabout, motorway joining or reverse parking at the supermarket.

You can learn in our dual-controlled car, or in your own car once you're comfortable (subject to your insurance).

Areas we cover

We pick up across Tolworth and around 6 miles of it, including Surbiton, Kingston, New Malden, Chessington, Worcester Park, Ewell, Esher and Morden. See all areas.

Frequently asked questions

How many refresher lessons will I need?

Many drivers feel confident again after three to six hours. We'll suggest a plan after your first lesson.

Can I use my own car?

Often, yes, once your instructor is happy. Your own insurance must cover you, and dual controls make the first lessons safer, so we usually start in our car.

Is there a test at the end?

No. Refresher lessons are about confidence and safety — there's no test unless you want a mock test for feedback.
