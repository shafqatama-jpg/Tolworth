Frequently asked questions

Why is the headline price £35 an hour?

£35 an hour is the hourly rate on our 30-hour block (£1,050). Pay-as-you-go lessons are £40 an hour.

Are prices different for automatic?

No. Every price on this page is the same for manual and automatic, in Tolworth and Morden.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes, with Klarna. We also accept all major debit and credit cards, including American Express. We never add card fees.

How long do block bookings last?

Block bookings must be used within 6 months of purchase. See our terms for cancellation and refund details.
