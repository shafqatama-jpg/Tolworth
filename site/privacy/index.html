Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Tolworth Driving School is part of Kings Driving Centre ("we", "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and complying with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

Information we collect

  • Full name and address
  • Phone number and email address
  • Driving licence number and eyesight confirmation
  • Pick-up and drop-off details
  • Theory and practical test information
  • Anything you choose to tell us in an enquiry, such as your experience or availability

How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange lessons
  • To schedule and manage your lessons and track your progress
  • To arrange practical tests and test cover
  • To contact you about schedule changes or road safety
  • To keep financial records as required by UK law (HMRC)

Our lawful bases are performing a contract with you (or taking steps at your request before one), our legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in running a safe driving school.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We use trusted service providers to run this website and handle enquiries: Netlify (website hosting), Formspree (delivering contact form messages to us) and Featurable (displaying our Google reviews). We may share information with DVSA where needed to arrange your test.

In-car cameras

For safety and training, our cars may be fitted with inward- and outward-facing dashcams. Recordings are used for internal training or insurance evidence and are deleted regularly.

Storage and retention

Information is stored on password-protected devices and secure cloud storage. We do not leave personal information in vehicles overnight. We keep accounting records for at least 7 years to meet legal obligations; other data is kept only as long as needed.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Email info@kingsdrivingcentre.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
