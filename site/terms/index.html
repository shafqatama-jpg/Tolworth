Terms and conditions

Last updated: November 2026

By booking a lesson with Tolworth Driving School (part of Kings Driving Centre), you agree to these terms. They help keep lessons safe and productive for everyone.

1. Licence and fitness to drive

  • You must hold a valid UK provisional or full driving licence and show it on your first lesson.
  • You must be medically fit to drive and meet the minimum eyesight standard (reading a number plate from 20 metres).
  • You must not be under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any medication that affects your driving.

2. Cancellations and payments

  • We need at least 48 hours' notice to cancel or rearrange a lesson. Otherwise, the full lesson fee is charged.
  • Individual lessons must be paid for at least 48 hours in advance or during the previous lesson.
  • Block bookings and intensive courses are non-refundable once the first 2 hours of training have been completed.
  • Block bookings must be used within 6 months of purchase.
  • We accept all major debit and credit cards, including American Express, and Klarna. We do not charge card fees.

3. Practical test and test cover

  • Your instructor may decline the use of the school car for a practical test if they believe your driving is not yet safe or test-ready.
  • We recommend a mock test at least 4 weeks before your test.

4. Dashcams

Our cars may be fitted with inward- and outward-facing dashcams for safety and training. See our privacy policy.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights as a consumer.
