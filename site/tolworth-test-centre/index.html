Tolworth Driving Test Centre: the local guide

What to expect on a driving test at Tolworth, the roads and junctions that catch learners out, and how to prepare — from instructors who teach here every day.

Book a lesson →WhatsApp us

Tolworth tests mix busy town driving around Tolworth Broadway with fast roads like the A3 Kingston bypass, large roundabouts and quiet residential streets for manoeuvres. The test lasts about 40 minutes. The best preparation is lessons and mock tests on these local roads.

What happens on the test

  1. Eyesight check — read a number plate from 20 metres.
  2. Tell me question — a vehicle safety question before you drive.
  3. General driving — on a mix of roads, often including dual carriageway.
  4. Independent driving — about 20 minutes following a sat nav or traffic signs.
  5. One manoeuvre — parallel park, bay park (in or out) or pull up on the right and reverse.
  6. Show me question — asked while you're driving, such as using the demister.

Roads and junctions to master

Tolworth Broadway

Shops, bus stops, pedestrian crossings and parked cars on both sides. Examiners watch for safe speed, good observation and correct positioning around buses and cyclists.

The A3 Kingston bypass and slip roads

Joining at speed is one of the biggest worries for Tolworth learners. Use the slip road to match the traffic speed, check mirrors and blind spot early, and merge smoothly. On leaving, plan your lane and speed in good time.

Tolworth and Hook roundabouts

Large, multi-lane roundabouts where the A3 meets local roads. Read the signs and road markings early, choose your lane on approach and stay in it. Hesitation and wrong lane choice are common faults.

Residential streets around Tolworth, Surbiton and Berrylands

Narrow roads with parked cars mean meeting traffic, giving way and choosing safe gaps. These are also the streets most used for manoeuvres.

Our top tips for passing at Tolworth

  • Drive the A3 slip roads until joining feels routine, not scary.
  • Practise independent driving with a sat nav — it's on every test.
  • Keep your speed steady and legal; Tolworth has frequent limit changes.
  • Take a mock test about four weeks before your test.
  • Have a warm-up lesson on the day so you arrive calm.

Test day checklist

  • Photocard provisional driving licence
  • Glasses or contact lenses if you need them
  • Arrive a few minutes early — we'll get you there in good time if you use our car

Pass rates for every test centre are published by DVSA on GOV.UK.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the driving test at Tolworth?

The practical car test lasts about 40 minutes, including around 20 minutes of independent driving following a sat nav or road signs.

What is the pass rate at Tolworth?

DVSA publishes pass rates for every test centre, updated regularly. Check the latest Tolworth figures on GOV.UK — and remember that preparation on local roads makes a bigger difference than the centre's average.

Do you know the exact Tolworth test routes?

DVSA no longer publishes official routes, and examiners vary them. We teach on the roads, junctions and roundabouts that come up most often around Tolworth, so nothing feels new on the day.

Can I use your car for my Tolworth test?

Yes — test cover is £130 for our pupils or £150 at short notice, including a warm-up lesson. You must be test-ready.

What happens if I fail?

Your examiner explains your faults. You can rebook after 10 working days. We'll focus your next lessons on exactly what went wrong.
